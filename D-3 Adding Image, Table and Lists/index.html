SUPERHEROES DETAILS

  • DEADPOOL a mercenary with regenerative healing powers and a twisted sense of humor.
  • SPIDERMAN a young superhero with spider-like abilities and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • IRONMAN a genius inventor and billionaire who fights crime in a high-tech suit of armor.
  • THOR the Norse god of thunder, wielding a powerful hammer and possessing superhuman strength.
  • HULK a scientist who transforms into a green-skinned behemoth with incredible strength when angered.
  • CAPTAIN AMERICA a super-soldier and patriotic hero who fights for justice and freedom.
  • WOLVERINE a mutant with retractable claws, enhanced senses, and a healing factor that makes him nearly indestructible.
  • MOONKNIGHT a vigilante with multiple identities and a connection to the Egyptian god Khonshu, using his skills to fight crime in the shadows.
