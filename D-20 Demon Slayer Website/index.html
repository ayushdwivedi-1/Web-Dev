FLAME BREATHING

Built upon intense passion, unyielding willpower, and explosive force, Flame Breathing mirrors the destructive, scorching energy of a raging fire. Unlike the fluid movements of Water Breathing, this style relies on singular, heavy-hitting strikes and singular, devastating slashes delivered from a stable, deeply rooted stance. The techniques are designed to completely overwhelm an opponent in a single bound, utilizing high-speed dashes that burn through defensive barriers. Its ultimate technique, the Ninth Form: Rengoku, allows the user to manifest a devastating, forward-charging slash that completely obliterates the surrounding terrain. It is a strictly offensive style passed down through the prestigious Rengoku family line, meant to instantly incinerate any threat.

WATER BREATHING

Renowned for being the most common, flexible, and beginner-friendly style, Water Breathing emphasizes fluidity, adaptability, and effortless momentum. Practitioners learn to mimic the flow of water, shifting seamlessly from soft, evasive deflections to overwhelming, crushing torrents depending on the flow of battle. It features ten standard forms, though the current Water Hashira, Giyu Tomioka, developed a legendary eleventh form called "Dead Calm" that neutralizes all incoming attacks instantly. Because it accommodates almost any physical build, it is the most widely taught style, prioritizing survival, defense, and capitalizing on the opponent's momentum rather than relying on pure brute strength.

WIND BREATHING

The most violent and hyper-aggressive of the elemental styles, Wind Breathing mimics the chaotic, destructive nature of a hurricane or a slicing gale. It focuses entirely on overwhelming, offensive pressure, utilizing acrobatics, leaps, and rapidly spinning slashes to create vacuum-like vortexes that shred anything within a wide radius. Practitioners like Sanemi Shinazugawa use it to unleash constant, sweeping strikes that act as both an absolute offense and a chaotic defense, deflecting incoming attacks with sheer kinetic wind pressure. It is a wildly erratic style that requires incredible agility and a relentless combat mindset, leaving the opponent completely suffocated under a barrage of air-cutting blades.

STONE BREATHING

The most physically demanding and grounded of all the core techniques, Stone Breathing draws its power directly from the earth to maximize physical durability and devastating impact. Unlike the other styles that primarily utilize traditional katanas, Stone Breathing is uniquely optimized for heavy, unconventional weapons—specifically a massive spiked flail and axe connected by a thick chain, as wielded by Gyomei Himejima. The forms emphasize absolute defense, crushing counterattacks, and utilizing the vibrations of the chain to map out the battlefield. By maintaining an unshakeable, rock-solid stance, a Stone Breathing master can withstand catastrophic impacts and deliver earth-shattering strikes capable of pulverizing even the hardest demon flesh.
